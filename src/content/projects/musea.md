Musea is an app I built to keep track of artworks you discover in museums and exhibitions. It lets you save photos of artworks with their title, artist, place, date, and tags, as well as keep a list of exhibitions you would like to visit.

Your collection stays on your device, works offline, and does not require an account. Instead of getting lost among the photos on your phone, the artworks become a personal archive that is easy to browse and revisit.
